# Creature Design - Bio-Mechanical Menagerie

## Design Principles

### Visual Style
- **Cardboard cutout** aesthetic with visible grain texture
- **Die-cut edges** with slight rounding
- **Plastic stands** in translucent colors matching creature type
- **Exposed mechanisms**: Gears, springs, pistons visible through "windows"
- **Painted metal** look: brass, copper, chrome, bronze finishes

### Naming Convention
- Technical/mechanical names with charm
- Format: `[Part/Action] + [Animal/Machine] + [Mark/Model]`
- Examples: "Tick-Tock Sparrow", "Coil Drake Mk.II", "Spring Hopper"

## Stat System

### Core Stats
- **Strength (STR)**: Physical power, damage output
- **Speed (SPD)**: Turn order, dodge chance
- **Intelligence (INT)**: Special abilities, strategy
- **Defense (DEF)**: Damage reduction
- **Stamina (STA)**: HP pool, endurance

### Derived Stats
- **HP** = Base + (STA × 10)
- **Attack** = Base + (STR × 2)
- **Evasion** = SPD × 0.5
- **Critical** = INT × 0.3

### Growth Curves
- Stats increase based on training activities
- Evolution gives stat multipliers
- Variants have different base stat distributions

## Color Coding System

### Material Finishes (indicate stat focus)
- **Chrome/Silver**: Speed-focused
- **Bronze/Brass**: Strength-focused
- **Copper/Gold**: Balanced
- **Steel/Iron**: Defense-focused
- **Colored metals**: Special element types

### Accent Colors (element type)
- **Red/Orange**: Fire/Steam
- **Blue/Aqua**: Water/Hydro
- **Yellow/White**: Electric
- **Green/Gray**: Kinetic/Neutral
- **Cyan/White**: Air/Pneumatic

## Animation Notes

### Idle Animations
- Gentle mechanical movements
- Visible gears rotating slowly
- Steam puffs / sparks / water drips
- Occasional blinks or looks around

### Movement
- Squash & stretch on start/stop
- Mechanical jerky quality with smooth interpolation
- Leave brief "exhaust" effects (steam, sparks, water trail)

### Emotions
- **Happy**: Bouncy movement, gears spin faster, bright eyes
- **Sad**: Slow down, dim lights, droopy posture
- **Excited**: Rapid vibration, sparks/steam increase
- **Tired**: Sputtering movement, occasional stops
